# Unhu Founders Feud — Host / Operator Guide

Everything you need to run the game live. No technical knowledge required. Read this once before the event and keep it open on a phone or second screen during the game.

---

## 1. Before the event (5-minute setup)

1. **Open the game** in a web browser (Chrome recommended) on the laptop that's plugged into the projector.
2. Click the **⛶ fullscreen** button (top-right) so it fills the whole screen.
3. Click anywhere once — this "unlocks" the sound (browsers require one click before audio can play). You'll hear effects after that.
4. Do a quick sound check: make sure laptop volume is up and the projector/speakers are working.

> **Tip:** The game auto-saves as you go. If the laptop refreshes, the browser reloads, or you accidentally close the tab, just reopen the page and it picks up **exactly where you left off** — same scores, same board.

---

## 2. Setting up the teams

The **Setup screen** opens first.

1. Enter the **number of teams** playing, then click **Set**.
2. A name box appears for each team — type each team's name (or leave the defaults like "Team 1").
3. Click **Save Teams**.
4. Click **Go to Game →** to reach the main board.

You can come back to Setup anytime to rename teams or add/remove them — scores are kept.

**How teams work:** Two teams play head-to-head on each question. Everyone else is the **audience** and can jump in during the "Open Floor" round (explained below). A live leaderboard of *all* teams is always on screen.

---

## 3. The game board — what you're looking at

- **Top center:** the current question.
- **Top right:** the 45-second **timer**.
- **Left panel:** the team playing on the left. **Right panel:** the team playing on the right.
  - Each shows their **score** and up to **3 strike marks (✗)**.
  - The team currently answering has a **gold highlight** and an "IN CONTROL" badge.
- **Center:** the hidden answer slots (numbered). You reveal these as teams guess correctly.
- **Bottom strip:** the always-visible **leaderboard** of every team.
- **Very bottom:** the **host control bar** — your buttons, grouped into 4 steps.

---

## 4. Running a question — step by step

The control bar is numbered **1 → 2 → 3 → 4** to match the flow.

### Step 1 · Set up the board
- Pick the **question** from the first dropdown.
- Pick the **left team** and **right team** (the two playing this round).
- Click **▶ Load This Board**.

### Step 2 · Play the round (the main part)
- Decide who answers first (coin toss, fastest buzz, whatever you like). Click **Control: [Team name]** to give that team control — their panel turns gold.
- When a team says a **correct answer**, click that answer slot on screen (it flips over with a "ding" and adds its points to the controlling team).
- When a team gives a **wrong answer**, click **✗ Wrong / Strike** — a big red X slams on screen with a buzzer. A team gets **3 strikes**, then control passes to the other team for one steal attempt (click the other **Control** button).
- Use **⏱ Timer** to start or reset the 45-second countdown as a guide. It won't end the round on its own — it's just for pacing.

### Step 3 · Open the floor to the audience (optional)
- Once the two playing teams are done (found the answers or struck out), click **🙋 Open Floor**.
- Now **any audience team** can claim the remaining hidden answers. First team to raise a hand wins the attempt:
  - Pick that team from the **Audience** dropdown.
  - Click the answer slot they got right — points go to that audience team.
- During Open Floor, the **whole audience shares ONE pool of 3 strikes**. After 3 wrong audience guesses, the board closes and any leftover answers are shown with no points.

### Step 4 · Wrap up and move on
- Click **Next Question ▶** to load the next board (scores carry over).
- **Reveal All** shows any remaining answers without giving points (handy to close out a board).
- **🏆 Leaderboard** opens the full standings on screen.
- **End Game** finishes the game and shows the **winner screen** with a celebration.

## 5. Keyboard shortcuts (optional, faster)

You can do everything with buttons, but these speed things up:

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **1–9** | Reveal answers 1 through 9 |
| **0** | Reveal answer 10 |
| **X** | Strike (wrong answer) |
| **O** | Open the floor to the audience |
| **T** | Start / reset the timer |
| **N** or **→** | Next question |
| **L** | Show / hide the full leaderboard |
| **M** | Mute / unmute sound |
| **?** | Show the shortcut help overlay |

> Press **?** anytime during the game to see this list on screen.

---

## 6. Scoring rules (quick reference)

- Each answer is worth a **fixed number of points** (shown when revealed) — higher-ranked answers are worth more.
- Correct answer → points go to whichever team is **in control** (or the chosen **audience team** during Open Floor).
- Points **accumulate across all questions**. The team with the most points at the end wins.
- Strikes don't subtract points — they just pass control after 3.

---

## 7. If something goes wrong (troubleshooting)

- **No sound?** Click anywhere on the page once (unlocks audio), check the **🔊** button isn't muted (top-right), and check laptop/projector volume.
- **Screen not full?** Click the **⛶** button top-right, or press your browser's fullscreen key (F11 on Windows, Ctrl+Cmd+F on Mac).
- **Clicked the wrong answer / gave points to the wrong team?** Reload the board for that question (Step 1 → **Load This Board**) to reset the current board's reveals. *Note: this resets the current question's board, not the overall leaderboard.*
- **Laptop refreshed / tab closed by accident?** Don't panic — reopen the page. Your session (scores and current board) is auto-saved and restores automatically.
- **Want to restart from scratch?** Go to the winner screen's **New Game** (resets all scores to zero, keeps teams and questions).
- **Text too small across the room?** The game is built for large screens — make sure you're in fullscreen and the projector resolution is reasonable. Standing back, everything should be readable.

---

## 8. Do's and don'ts

**Do**
- Run it in **fullscreen** on the projector laptop.
- Click once at the start to enable sound.
- Keep this laptop plugged into power — don't let it sleep mid-game.

**Don't**
- Don't switch browser tabs/windows during a dramatic reveal (it's fine, just less smooth).
- Don't clear the browser data mid-event — that's where the auto-save lives.

---

That's it. The flow is always: **Load board → give control → reveal correct answers / strike wrong ones → open the floor → next question → end game.** Have fun running it!
